## Project

Design a website for **Palma Pool Club**, a seasonal outdoor pool venue on Kosmaj hill, ~30 minutes from Belgrade, Serbia. It has two pools, a jacuzzi, sun loungers, a branded food truck ("Palma Pool Cafe"), and hosts ticketed "Matine" DJ pool-party events on weekends. Tagline: **"Sunce. Palma. Pogled."** (Sun. Palm. View.) All copy should be in **Serbian**, using the direct, sunny, short-sentence tone of their existing marketing (e.g. "☀️🌴 Palma Pool Club je bazen sa predivnim pogledom, u mirnom predelu Kosmaja, na nekih 30 minuta od Beograda.").

Design references: draw the warm, photography-forward, editorial feeling of **skyfallbelgrade.com** (warm terracotta accent on white/cream, big hero photography, gallery with a day/night toggle) and the clean typographic menu layout of **infinitypoolhouse.rs** (elegant dotted-leader price lists). Palma's own real photography (aerial drone shots, a striking red-and-white polka-dot mushroom pool float, a hillside valley view) is stronger than either reference — let it lead.

## Brand & Style System

**Two visual modes, used deliberately by section:**

- **Daytime mode** (Home, Pricing, Menu, Gallery, FAQ, Location): bright, sunny, warm. Base neutrals: warm sand/limestone (like the venue's actual pool deck), soft cream. Primary accent: a rich pool teal/aqua. Secondary accent: warm brass/gold. Photography-led — big, confident use of real aerial and poolside shots.
- **Nightlife mode** (Events / Matine page only): gold-on-black, matching their actual DJ flyer branding — deep near-black background, warm gold/brass text and rules, palm-tree mark rendered in gold, energetic and premium rather than corporate.

**Typography**: a warm display serif with some character (not a generic geometric sans) for headings, paired with a clean humanist sans for body text and data-heavy content (pricing/menu tables need to read cleanly with tabular numerals). Uppercase tracked labels for eyebrows/section tags.

**Logo**: a palm tree silhouette over a sun/wave motif in a circular badge, with "PALMA" wordmark below in a confident geometric sans, and "POOL CLUB" in a smaller tracked-out label underneath.

**Motion**: this should feel premium and alive, not static — parallax on hero imagery as the user scrolls, scroll-triggered reveals for content sections, subtle hover states on cards/buttons (not just color-change — slight lift/scale).

**Imagery to reference conceptually**: aerial drone shots of two pools with sun loungers and umbrellas; a circular jacuzzi shot from directly overhead with a swimmer; a wide landscape shot of the terrace with a pergola and valley view; a red-and-white polka-dot mushroom-shaped pool float (a recurring, almost mascot-like visual element); a branded yellow food truck labeled "PALMA POOL CAFE"; a gold-on-black DJ event poster style for nightlife content.

## SCREEN 1 — Home

Sticky nav: logo left, links (Home, Pricing, Menu, Events, Gallery, FAQ, Location), a filled **"Rezerviši" (Book Now)** button on the right, teal/brass accent.

Hero: full-bleed real aerial pool photograph, tagline "Sunce. Palma. Pogled." large in the display serif, one-line subhead about the venue, two CTAs ("Rezerviši svoj ležaj" primary, "Pogledaj cenovnik" secondary).

Quick-facts strip beneath hero: hours (10:00–19:00, svaki dan), distance ("43 km / 30 min od Beograda"), phone (click-to-call).

Amenities section: 4–6 cards/tiles — Dva bazena, Jacuzzi sa pogledom, Palma Pool Cafe (food truck), Stoni tenis & bilijar, DJ žurke (Matine) — each with a short line and an icon or photo crop.

Pricing teaser: compact version of the weekday/weekend table (Radnim danima 2.500 din / Vikendom 3.500 din, deca 4–12: 1.250 / 1.750 din, deca do 4: besplatno) with a "Pun cenovnik →" link.

Gallery teaser: 4–6 image grid pulling from the curated real photos, "Pogledaj galeriju →".

Upcoming Matine teaser: dark gold-on-black card previewing the next event, breaking the daytime palette on purpose as a visual hook toward the Events page.

Map teaser + footer: address, phone, Instagram link, hours, nav links repeated.
